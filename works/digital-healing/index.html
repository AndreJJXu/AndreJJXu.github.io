Competition project · Digital therapeutics

Digital Healing (数智疗愈): a Support Platform for Subthreshold Depression.

A research-translation project: the lab's affective-computing work, turned into a daily support service concept for the population just below the clinical threshold.

CICSC 2026 · Higher-Education Main Track Graduate Creative Group Digital therapeutics Multimodal affective computing

Project lead — Junjie Xu · team at East China Normal University

Subthreshold depression is widespread — especially among young adults — yet it sits below the clinical threshold where treatment resources concentrate. Digital Healing pairs multimodal affective assessment with emotion-controllable music and visual generation to offer low-threshold daily support: two product concepts (guided digital meditation, music–visual healing) on a five-layer platform, entered into the 2026 China International College Students' Innovation Competition as project lead.

01

The gap

Subthreshold depression — depressive symptoms that do not reach a clinical diagnosis — affects a large population, with young adults hit hardest. It is precisely this group that the current system serves worst: clinical resources concentrate on diagnosed patients, while what is offered below the threshold is mostly self-report trackers and canned content, with no generative, personally responsive support.

The project starts from a research bet this lab has been making for years: if models can assess affect multimodally and generate emotion-controlled music and imagery, those capabilities can compose into a daily support service — not by replacing clinicians, but by catching the space between journaling and therapy.

02

The approach

The platform is organized as a closed loop across five layers. Sensing collects multimodal signals; fusion forms an affective assessment; decision selects the support strategy; execution generates the intervention content; feedback closes the loop by measuring response and adjusting.

The execution layer is where the lab's research lines converge: emotion-controllable text-to-music generation and music-to-image generation with affective intent — the same alignment and preference-learning questions studied in the lab's publication line, here composed into a service pipeline rather than a benchmark.

An ethics-approved pilot study at a university psychology center (371 participants, Dec 2025 – Aug 2026) informed the requirement design: what people below the threshold actually experience, respond to, and drop.

03

The products

Two concepts carry the loop to users. Guided digital meditation (数智冥想) adapts session pacing and sonic texture to the assessed state. Music–visual healing (音画疗愈) turns a user's emotional state into a personalized piece of music and matching generated imagery — support that is made for the moment rather than retrieved from a library.

The interfaces follow one principle: assessment stays ambient, generation stays legible. The user always sees what the system inferred and what it made, in plain form.
