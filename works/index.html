01 Benchmark & audit

Evidence Before Severity

Under review · AAAI 2027

A 21,628-evaluation same-text audit of whether LLM safety judgments in depression-related dialogues follow expressed evidence — or just severity cues. Includes a 29-source, 869,268-record Evidence Atlas and an eight-configuration robustness extension.

02 Evaluation framework

EviDial

In preparation · ICLR 2027

A panel-assessability framework for judging LLM evaluators on depression-related dialogues: 1,910 blind adjudications at 100% coverage, five-protocol judge panels, and evidence that protocol diversity rivals model-family diversity.

Open project page →
1,910 adjudications · 5 protocol panels
CEF. stability · direction · no free lunch

03 Theory program

Counterfactual Evidence Fidelity

Exploratory program · ICLR 2027 target

A theory-first program defining evidence fidelity as three observable behavioral tenets, audited across 42,240 exploratory conditions — with a mechanism candidate that moves quadruplet pass from 0.83 to 1.0000, and an honest cross-model null.

Open project page →

04 Agent safety

ANI — Affective Non-Interference

Phase 2 in progress · ICLR 2027 target

A preregistered, two-phase agent-safety study: 6,480 confirmatory episodes returned an honest NONE_CONFIRMED for affect generalization — and exposed an affect-independent policy-compliance fragility (282/288 violations). Phase 2 scales to multi-agent composite attacks.

Open project page →
6,480 episodes · preregistered · two phases

05 Provenance audit

Before the Score

Under review · ICLR 2027

A provenance-based audit across 129 news-interaction runs: identical evaluator messages under different target labels, 313 temporal contrasts with unchanged target text, and 79/85 runs repeating a rise-then-fall score pattern — with zero new model calls.
