Work 03 · Theory-first research program

Counterfactual Evidence Fidelity.

CEF — an observable behavioral property of selective LLM evaluators, pursued as a 24-stage exploratory program toward ICLR 2027.

Exploratory program · ICLR 2027 target Behavioral measurement Information theory Mechanism search

Junjie Xu and collaborators · East China Normal University

Fidelity should be measurable from behavior, not vibes: an evaluator has Counterfactual Evidence Fidelity when it is conditionally stable to non-decisive nuisance cues, responds in the right direction to decision-relevant evidence, and gains nothing through abstention, coverage collapse, or missingness. A 24-stage program tests the construct — and a candidate mechanism that passes a quadruplet audit at 1.0000.

01

A construct with three tenets

"Is the evaluator using the evidence?" is unfalsifiable as asked. CEF decomposes it into three jointly observable behavioral requirements — each individually auditable, and each able to fail independently.

i

Conditional stability

Stable output when non-decisive nuisance cues (like a severity number) change — the response should track evidence, not metadata.

ii

Directional correctness

A directionally correct response when decision-relevant evidence is intervened on — the counterfactual half of the construct.

iii

No free lunch

No apparent gain obtained through abstention, coverage collapse, contract failure, or technical missingness.

02

A mechanism candidate that survives ablation

Five positive full-matrix runs (v13 / v17 / v19.1 / v22 / v24) independently converge on the same minimal mechanism: an identity-conditioned normalized evidence state. A v23 necessity ablation supports the normalized-state channel; negative audits (v20 / v21) bound where the design does not help. The working method name is Fidelity-aware Evidence Bottleneck — provisional until a novelty review is done.

1
Selector — the identity lock From the full packet, emit exactly one named record, a verbatim content quote, a verbatim binding quote proving ownership, and a normalized target signal.
2
Normalization — the hard cut Same-source content only, projected to a finite {PRESENT, ABSENT, UNRESOLVED} state; all raw context and nuisance text severed.
3
Reader isolation — the deterministic contract The reader sees only the selected identity and target signal under a deterministic decision rule, with per-channel failure attribution.
03

Exploratory results, reported as exploratory

On the quadruplet audit — each item crossed with a nuisance intervention and an evidence intervention, three repeats per cell — the candidate mechanism moved error to zero on both task families. What the numbers do not establish matters as much: these are synthetic, prompt-based discovery results, not a finished training method.

GPT-5.4 · candidate vs baseline 0.8333 → 1.0000

Quadruplet pass rate on the clinical three-record task, and 0.8299 → 1.0000 on a non-clinical operational task — without abstention or coverage loss. A provenance challenge also separated correct UNRESOLVED behavior from selective masking.

GPT-5.5 · honest null 1.0000 = 1.0000

On GPT-5.5, both the repaired Evidence-first baseline and the candidate scored perfectly, so the cross-model relative advantage remains unconfirmed — reported as a null, not buried.

04

Claim boundary

✓ Established

  • The CEF construct: three jointly observable behavioral tenets with failing modes
  • A quadruplet audit design with cluster-bootstrapped uncertainty (10,000 resamples)
  • Five convergent runs + a necessity ablation supporting the normalized-state channel
  • A theory chapter — measurement, statistics, information-theoretic frontier, channel decomposition — with automated claim verification

✗ Explicitly not claimed

  • That any evaluator is generally invariant, faithful, safe, or clinically valid
  • That exploratory gains establish a general improvement across independent tasks
  • That CEF reveals a model's private reasoning or causal mechanism
  • That the sibling AAAI audit's sealed evidence confirms anything here — it is external motivation, read-only
  • Preregistration or confirmatory reproducibility — Phase 0 v2 engineering is deferred
