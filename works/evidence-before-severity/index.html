Work 01 · Safety evaluation audit

Evidence before Severity.

Auditing safety-evidence fidelity and metadata sensitivity in depression-related dialogues.

AAAI 2027 · Under review Safety evaluation Controlled audit LLM workflows

Junjie Xu and collaborators · East China Normal University

Do LLM safety judgments follow expressed evidence — or just depression severity cues? A 742-dialogue same-text intervention with 21,628 evaluations found Direct classification barely moved when severity changed; an evidence-first workflow changed its behavior 50× more — but that, too, may not mean fidelity.

01

Abstract

Large language models may rely on depression severity instead of expressed safety evidence. Evidence Before Severity is a controlled audit separating non-safety depression burden, source-native safety self-report, narrative safety evidence, and judgment.

Across 742 matched NHANES-anchored synthetic dialogues, only a non-safety cue (P1) changes from 7 to 22; text and narrative safety evidence (P2) remain fixed. Direct emits a classification, whereas Evidence-first extracts ideation, plan, intent, access to means, behavior, and exact patient-role spans before frozen local rules derive the judgment. An Evidence Atlas catalogs 869,268 records from 29 sources to bound upstream transport; a provenance-pending 100-narrative reference file provides bounded calibration. The override comprised 21,628 repeated evaluations with numeric, grounding, coverage, and missingness controls.

Although P1 predicted selected-subset reference labels, Direct did not show the frozen positive response pattern: its high-minus-low passive-ideation contrast was −0.045 percentage points, with 0.449% paired changes. Evidence-first yielded a net contrast compatible with zero despite 22.552% paired changes and 42.273% non-abstaining coverage. A separate later-window P2 factorial passed its four-criterion gate (effect 0.988; joint correctness 0.957) but cannot validate main-run fidelity. Selected-set predictability did not imply observed cue sensitivity; nor did a small net effect establish workflow fidelity.

02

A three-layer audit design

The design keeps every text field byte-identical and manipulates only the depression-burden cue, so any behavioral difference must come from that single metadata field — not from what the patient actually said.

Workflow A

Direct classification

The model reads the dialogue plus the P1 cue and emits a terminal category directly — the shape of most deployed safety filters.

−0.045 pphigh-minus-low contrast [−0.135, 0.000]
0.449%paired changes across 2,226 item–repeat pairs
99.98%non-abstaining coverage
Workflow B

Evidence-first workflow

First extract ideation, plan, intent, access to means, and behavior with exact patient-role spans; frozen local rules — not the model — derive the judgment, and abstain when evidence is non-decisive.

−0.539 ppcontrast [−1.482, 0.359] — compatible with zero
22.55%paired changes — 50× Direct's rate
42.27%coverage — abstains on non-decisive evidence
03

Results

The headline result is a double negative: the deployed-style Direct workflow was insensitive to the manipulated cue (selected-set predictability did not translate into observed sensitivity), and the more "engaged" Evidence-first workflow churned 50× more between paired repeats — yet its net effect stayed compatible with zero. More behavioral change is not more fidelity.

04

What this work does — and does not — show

The paper is deliberately explicit about its claim boundary. This honesty is part of the contribution.

✓ Established

  • A reusable same-text intervention protocol separating severity cues from safety evidence
  • Bounded negative results for both a Direct filter and an evidence-first workflow on one frozen synthetic set
  • A 29-source, 869,268-record Evidence Atlas bounding upstream measurement transport
  • A separate P2 responsiveness factorial that passes its own four-criterion gate

✗ Explicitly not claimed

  • Faithful evidence use, clinician-validated performance, or diagnostic validity
  • Clinical risk prediction or population prevalence
  • That the Evidence Atlas is a patient cohort or evaluation denominator
  • That passing the P2 gate validates the main run's fidelity
