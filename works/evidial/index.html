Work 02 · Benchmark & measurement

EviDial.

Evidence + Dialogue — a panel-assessability framework for judging LLM evaluators on depression-related dialogues.

In preparation · ICLR 2027 Evaluation benchmark LLM judge panels Clinical dialogue

Junjie Xu and collaborators · East China Normal University

What makes a depression-dialogue utterance assessable in the first place? EviDial replaces an unaffordable human gold standard with a five-protocol LLM judge panel — 1,910 adjudications at 100% coverage and zero cash cost — and shows that protocol diversity rivals model-family diversity (effective judge count 1.59 vs 1.56).

01

Panel-assessability, not borrowed ground truth

Clinical dialogue evaluation usually assumes a human gold standard. At ~¥175k and 4–6 months for 3,200 anchored adjudications, that assumption prices out most labs. EviDial takes a different route: the construct under measurement is panel-assessability — whether a five-protocol judge panel, after intervention-based calibration, can render a stable, auditable verdict on an utterance — and the paper explicitly does not claim clinical equivalence for the panel consensus.

The frozen suffneg_pass fix shows why the machinery matters: an always-positive system scored a perfect 72/72 = 1.0 under the legacy metric, and exactly 0/72 = 0.0 under the joint metric. Eight of eight captures held under frozen tolerance.

02

Findings from 3,000+ blind adjudications

The M1 full-panel run delivered 1,910/1,910 adjudications at 100% coverage with zero cash cost; the M2 stratified extension added 205 utterances × 5 protocols = 1,025 more. The pilot line established the quantitative core: a same-base-model protocol panel reaches an effective judge count of 1.59 vs 1.56 for a five-family model panel — protocol diversity is a viable substitute for model diversity.

Claim boundary

✓ Established so far

  • A frozen task contract, evidence-state schema, and metric spec with automated checkers
  • 1,910 + 1,025 blind panel adjudications at full coverage, frozen protocols, blinded dialogue text
  • A mechanism experiment isolating binding-specific distractor interference
  • A unified results manifest (84 rows) with conflict reporting, three gates green
  • An independent human-annotation harness, handbook, and cost model, ready to run

✗ Not claimed (yet)

  • Human-anchored gold standard — the 2–3-annotator study (~196 hours) is specified but pending
  • A real five-method judge comparison — blocked on API credentials
  • Clinical equivalence of panel consensus to expert judgment
  • Conference acceptance — internal simulated review scores are engineering feedback, not results
  • An early construct-validity finding was retracted when the monotonic rise proved to be symptom burden, not assessability
