Publication 03 · Music-to-visual emotion

Aesthetic Matters in Music Perception for Image Stylization.

An emotion-driven music-to-visual manipulation method — musical elements become emotional image edits, evaluated down to EEG responses.

Preprint · arXiv 2025 Music-to-visual Emotion-driven EEG evaluation

Junjie Xu, Xingjiao Wu, Tanren Yao, Zihao Zhang, Jiayang Bei, Wu Wen, Liang He · East China Normal University

Music carries emotion that text captions flatten. EmoMV treats musical emotion as a control signal for image stylization: a bottom-up stage reads pitch, rhythm, and harmony into emotion, and a top-down stage writes that emotion into visual attributes like color and lighting. A multi-scale evaluation — image quality, aesthetic judgment, and real-time EEG — checks that what changed in the music is what viewers feel in the image.

01

The question

Cross-modal systems map environmental sound to concrete sources — traffic, footsteps. Music is different: abstract, layered, and emotionally dynamic, with no ground-truth image to recover. Existing audio-visual work largely skips this emotional dimension.

The question: can the emotional content of music be extracted from its structural elements, and applied to an image in a way viewers actually feel?

02

The approach

EmoMV mirrors human cognition in two directions. Bottom-up: fundamental structural elements of music — pitch, rhythm, chord changes — are composed into an emotional representation. Top-down: that emotion is embodied into aesthetic low-level dimensions — light, exposure, color — as image manipulation. A Mus-Vis Textual Alignment module harmonizes musical and visual descriptions; an Emotion-aware Aesthetic Image Refinement module applies the edit.

03

What it shows

Qualitative results show the same image stylized differently under different musical emotions — harmony shifts read as color and lighting shifts, not arbitrary filters.

The multi-scale evaluation combines image quality metrics, aesthetic assessment, and EEG measurements that track real-time emotional responses — giving the subjective side of the claim an objective instrument.

04

Claim boundary

✓ Established

  • A two-stage, cognitively-motivated music-to-visual manipulation design
  • Alignment of musical and visual emotional descriptions before editing
  • A multi-scale evaluation including real-time EEG, beyond automatic metrics
  • An honest scope: image stylization driven by musical emotion

✗ Explicitly not claimed

  • EEG supports group-level response, not per-image mind reading
  • No claim of universal emotional ground truth across listeners
  • Preprint — results are preliminary until peer review
  • No therapeutic-efficacy claim (see ElysianMV for the therapeutic line)
