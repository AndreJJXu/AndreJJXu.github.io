Publication 11 · Music-guided generation

MusePainter: Cross-modal Alignment and Human Preference Learning.

Music is abstract, layered, and subjective — so alignment must be structured and preference must be disentangled. This is the CM-Align line, under the name MusePainter.

Manuscript · MusePainter Music-to-image Multi-axis RLHF User study

Junjie Xu, Xingjiao Wu, and collaborators · East China Normal University

Mapping evolving musical affect into coherent imagery breaks two assumptions at once: that a ground truth exists (listeners differ), and that a single reward captures quality (they differ in several directions). MusePainter extracts structured descriptors of the music — structural, stylistic, affective — and optimizes three preference axes independently: emotional consistency, semantic alignment, creative appeal. It surpasses audio-to-image and audio→text→image baselines in semantic fidelity, stylistic congruence, and affective resonance.

01

The question

Environmental sounds have concrete sources; music has moods that evolve across melody, rhythm, and harmony. Mapping that into a static image demands structured representations — and no universal ground truth can exist, because the same melody suggests different imagery to different listeners. Objective metrics such as CLIP or FAD cannot fully reflect emotional resonance.

The question: how do you make generation controllable when the target itself is subjective — without collapsing subjectivity into one scalar reward?

02

The approach

MusePainter first deconstructs music into fine-grained attributes — melody, rhythm, harmony, instrumentation, overall structure — captured as structured descriptors across structural, stylistic, and affective dimensions. Generation is then optimized with a multi-axis preference scheme that disentangles emotional consistency, semantic alignment, and creative appeal, and optimizes each independently. The music understanding side is a two-stage fine-tuned audio-LLM (MuseAes-LLM), with descriptor lengths balanced (20/50/70 tokens) to trade hallucination against detail.

03

What it shows

On curated benchmarks, MusePainter surpasses strong audio-to-image and audio→text→image baselines in semantic fidelity, stylistic congruence, and affective resonance. The qualitative comparisons show the three axes separating cleanly: the same piece can be steered toward accuracy, toward style, or toward mood without retraining.

User studies corroborate the automatic metrics — participants prefer MusePainter outputs along the axes the optimization targeted.

04

Claim boundary

✓ Established

  • Structured music-theoretic descriptors as controllable guidance
  • Multi-axis preference optimization: emotional consistency, semantic alignment, creative appeal
  • Superior semantic fidelity, stylistic congruence, and affective resonance vs strong baselines
  • Components designed to generalize beyond music-to-image

✗ Explicitly not claimed

  • Withdrawn from ICLR 2026; under revision — not a reviewed result
  • Preference labels reflect recruited raters, not a universal audience
  • No claim of real-time generation or deployment
  • Cross-modal extension of the components is proposed, not proven
